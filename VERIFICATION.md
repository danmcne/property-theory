## Method

- **Single source.** `source/theory.py` holds every axiom, definition, theorem and scenario. `source/build.py` parses and type-checks each formula and generates every TPTP file. No formula is maintained by hand in two places.
- **Entailment (unbounded domains).** Each theorem has a minimal problem that contains only the axioms, definitions and earlier theorems (lemmas) listed for it in `minimal/MANIFEST.md`. Vampire 5.1.0 proves the TFF version; Leo-III 1.7.18, with E 3.5.1 as its first-order back end, proves the THF version. A theorem counts as proved only if both report `SZS status Theorem`. The lemma graph is checked to be acyclic.
- **Consistency.** Three independent routes: (1) the story world in `MODEL.md`, written by hand and evaluated against every formula by a plain-Python evaluator; (2) Vampire's finite model builder, whose models are parsed and re-evaluated by the same evaluator; (3) Z3 over a grounded finite domain.
- **Scenarios (Z3).** Concrete situations with an expected outcome; a 'forced' conclusion is checked by asserting its negation and obtaining unsat. Unsat results hold for the stated finite domain; their general forms are theorems.
- **Non-vacuity (Vampire fmb).** For each theorem `![X]: (H => C)`, the full theory plus `?[X]: H` has a finite model, re-checked by the evaluator. No theorem holds only because its hypothesis is impossible.

## Known limits of the verification

- Leo-III alone (no E back end, 120 s per problem) proves 45 of the 55 theorems; it does not finish on `endless_past_paradox`, `fight_one_aggressor`, `share_gift`, `joint_purchase`, `abandoner_not_owner`, `lease_tenant_excludes_landlord`, `lessor_loses_manner`, `res_nullius`, `life_cycle`, `sole_owner_controls`.
- Finite model checks prove satisfiability outright. They do not prove that the theory has models with infinitely many instants. Over a time order with no first instant (for example the integers) and a person who uses and claims an object at every instant, the title axioms have no model; see THEORY.md, 'Time'.
- The compossibility argument in THEORY.md is a proof sketch, not a machine-checked theorem.
